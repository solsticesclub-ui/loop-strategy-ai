Loop Strategy AI is a dual-service consultancy for luxury property developers, founded by Fanny — a Belgian architect specialized in sustainable design with years of experience on luxury development projects in Belgium, Italy and Spain. The two services are Visual Intelligence (photorealistic renders delivered in 48 hours from photos, sketches, SketchUp models or floor plans, without a CGI studio) and AI Operating System (a custom AI implementation built for your specific development pipeline in three weeks, your team operates it autonomously after delivery). Serves developers, investors and architects across Belgium, France, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Switzerland, Monaco, the UK and the UAE.
